Vault.ai
Products Suite

One Platform for Identity, Compliance and Operations

Vault.ai is a product suite for India's lenders, fintechs and remittance providers, covering verification, transaction risk and case management.

Product Suite

Three products that cover the full lifecycle, from onboarding a customer to reviewing their transactions.

Launching Q2 2026
Verify
Identity Verification Infrastructure
Purpose

Help lenders, fintech companies and digital banks verify customer identities and onboard users with less manual review.

Capabilities
  • Aadhaar verification
  • PAN verification
  • OCR and document intelligence
  • Liveness detection
  • Name matching
  • Fraud and spoofing checks
  • Verification workflow automation
Customers: Lenders, NBFCs, Fintechs, Digital banks
Next
Comply
Transaction Risk and Compliance Infrastructure
Purpose

Help remittance providers and payment companies assess transaction risk before money moves.

Capabilities
  • Sanctions screening
  • PEP screening
  • Adverse media monitoring
  • Beneficiary verification
  • Transaction risk scoring
  • Corridor risk assessment
  • Compliance decision engine
Customers: Remittance providers, cross-border payment companies, fintechs handling international payments
Next
Flow
Operations and Case Management Platform
Purpose

Help operations, risk and compliance teams manage reviews, escalations and investigations.

Capabilities
  • Manual review queues
  • Case management
  • Escalation workflows
  • Audit logs
  • Reporting
  • Compliance copilot
  • Investigator dashboard
Customers: Compliance teams, risk teams, operations teams

How the products fit together

Verify confirms who the customer is. Comply assesses the risk of each transaction that customer makes. Flow gives teams a single place to review, escalate and audit the cases that either product raises.
